Paste the **PROMPT** block below into Lyria 3 Pro (Gemini). The lyrics are inside it.
The video is 5:10, so if Lyria gives you less (usually ~2-3 min), generate it twice
(Part 1 + Part 2 prompts below) or loop the outro.

## Tips
- If the first result is not Brazilian enough, add: "authentic Rio de Janeiro baile funk
  drum pattern, Brazilian Portuguese vocals with carioca accent".
- If vocals sound too clean, add: "raw, close-mic, gritty, slightly distorted vocals".
- Sync tip: the domain clash starts at 2:10 in the video (clip 14), the domain shatters at
  2:50 (clip 18), the last punch is at 4:20 (clip 27), and Sukuna stands alone from 4:50.
